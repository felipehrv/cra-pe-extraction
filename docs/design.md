## 1. Purpose and framing

Bank regulators (OCC, FDIC, Federal Reserve) publish a Community Reinvestment Act Performance Evaluation (PE) after each exam. The overall rating is available in structured form, but much of what the PE says exists only in prose and tables: component test ratings (for two of three regulators), whether examiners identified discriminatory or other illegal credit practices and whether that lowered the rating, the laws involved, how many assessment areas the bank has, which loan products were evaluated, the share of lending inside the assessment areas, and the loan-to-deposit ratio. Nobody publishes these as data.

The project builds a pipeline that extracts these fields from every Small Bank and Intermediate Small Bank (ISB) PE released in 2023 to 2025, measures how far each extracted value can be trusted, estimates what extraction costs at scale, finds errors in the regulators' own structured feeds, and ends with a short memo on what regulators should publish in structured form so a pipeline like this would not be needed.

The README opens with the problem, not with the domain. The CRA is the application; the subject is verified extraction from official records. One sentence in the README states: "This project measures what the documents say; it is not an enforcement scorecard."

Hard constraints: no data or code from the author's current employer projects; everything in the repo is public government material or generated by the repo's own code.

## 2. Corpus and index

- Population: OCC, FDIC and FRB PEs, exam method Small Bank or ISB, public release date 2023-01-01 to 2025-12-31. The count is taken from the manifest once built; the prior estimate is 2,600 to 2,800.
- Index sources (verified in the data check of 2026-10-05):
  - OCC: JSON API behind the occ.gov CRA search (`POST .../api/CraEvaluations/Search/Options`), fields Charter, BankName, Rating, Exam (method), eDte, pDte, pdf. Filter to release date in window; the 2023-2025 pull also contains 2026 rows and 2 blank-release rows, which are logged as excluded. PDF URL = `occ.gov/static/cra/craeval` + `pdf` field, used verbatim (folder names are mixed case and not derivable from dates).
  - FDIC: CRAPES API (`POST crapes.fdic.gov/api/getSearchResults`), results capped at 500 rows per query, so pulls are by month and method with an assertion that no query returns 500. `SYS_EXTRN_FILE_ID` has the pattern `YYYY/cert_YYMMDD.PDF`; the YYMMDD is parsed as `exam_date_candidate` and verified against the printed evaluation date on gold. PDF URL = `crapes.fdic.gov/publish/{SYS_EXTRN_FILE_ID}`. The release month is the only date in the API otherwise.
  - FRB: CSV download from the CRA public web app after a search (session cookie). Rows are deduplicated on (IDRSSD, exam date); the row with blank MSA/State is the main row, the others are stored in `data/index/frb_state_rows.csv`. Method label for ISB is "Int Small Bank". ISB main rows carry Lending and Community Development ratings. PDF URL = `federalreserve.gov/apps/CRAPubWeb/CRA/DownloadPDF/{RSSD}_{YYYYMMDD}`.
  - Prior-exam lookup: index rows pulled back to 2018 for OCC and FDIC, all years for FRB, to verify `prior_evaluation_date` and `prior_rating`.
  - FFIEC's combined ratings file is not used (CAPTCHA on download, stale as of mid-2024, FDIC dates are release months). It is cited in the data-quality table.
- Manifest `config/corpus.csv`: doc_id, regulator, bank_id, bank_name, exam_date, release_date, method, overall_rating, url, sha256, pages, chars_per_page, text_extractable, split.
- Fetching: `make fetch` with rate limiting, a User-Agent, retries and resume; sha256 verified. The gold-set PDFs (about 40 files) and their text are committed to the repo so evaluation runs offline; the rest are fetched on demand. PEs are US government works.
- Parsing: pymupdf (or pdfplumber if tables need it), page-wise text split on form feed. `text_extractable` is false when characters per page fall below a threshold; counts by regulator are reported. OCR (tesseract) is implemented only if more than 2 percent of the corpus is scanned; all 8 sample PDFs were born-digital.

## 3. Field groups and data dictionary

Fields are grouped by how they are scored, not by difficulty.

- Group A: scored on the full corpus against the agency indexes (exact external truth for every document).
- Group B: scored on the gold test set; corpus-level error estimated by a stratified audit.
- Group C: the illegal-credit-practices finding, handled as a census of every document's review section, not as a sampled field.
- Group D (stretch): per-assessment-area home-mortgage geographic distribution rows.

Gold null vocabulary: `value`, `not_applicable` (NA), `absent_from_document` (ABS). System output vocabulary: `value`, `absent`, `abstain`. Applicability of each field is a function of (regulator, exam method) and every applicability rule cites the document that established it.

Removed from v0 and why: the bank-level home-mortgage LMI share is a computed quantity (sum over per-AA tables, pooled years, narrative only for FRB) and cannot satisfy the verbatim-quote rule by construction; it is replaced by the inside-AA share and the loan-to-deposit ratio, both printed as a single number in all 8 samples, and kept as the stretch per-AA table (Group D). The HMDA cross-check is reduced to one worked paragraph in the memo (SouthStar, Austin AA: the PE's 243 loans and LMI counts reproduce only when HMDA purchases are included and the AA counties are used instead of the MSA). `HMDA` is removed from the laws enum (it is a data source, not an illegal credit practice).

## 4. Extraction systems

- S0 keyword baseline: sentence-level patterns with negation handling ("lowered", "violation(s) of", "identified ... illegal credit practice" not preceded by "has not", "did not", "no evidence"). It mirrors what an analyst would run. Scored against the census; false-positive rate reported, OCC separately (the bare phrase appears several times in every OCC PE).
- S1 rules: page-wise, scope-aware locator (every page or span tagged institution, state, assessment_area, appendix, toc; table-of-contents lines skipped) with a per-regulator heading table in config and a pytest case per sample document; regex for cover page, rating sentences, description-of-institution numerics; table parser for the AA concentration and LTD tables. Versioned with a changelog.
- S3 LLM via the Claude API: structured outputs (`output_config.format` built from `fields.yaml`), input is the located sections (whole-document fallback when the locator misses, with the fallback rate reported), effort fixed and recorded, model id and the response's model field and call date recorded per trace. No temperature parameter exists on current models and thinking cannot be disabled, so reproducibility is by trace replay, not by sampling settings; run-to-run agreement is measured by running dev twice (never test).
- S4 open-weight model run locally (Llama 3.1 8B instruct or Qwen2.5 7B instruct, Q4, via Ollama, `num_ctx` set explicitly with an assertion that prompt tokens fit): same prompts and grounding checks as S3. Scope: all gold documents plus a 100-document random corpus sample with wall-clock and hardware recorded; the full corpus if throughput allows. If the author's machine cannot run it, the README's Limitations say so rather than removing the comparison silently.
- S5 combined: S1 for a Group A field when its agreement with the index on the corpus split (test excluded) is at least 98 percent with coverage at least 95 percent; S3 otherwise, with S1 as a consistency flag. The routing rule is pre-specified.
- Not a system: a distilled TF-IDF classifier for the finding field. With an expected 20 to 60 positives in the corpus it is not trainable, and the screener role is already filled by S0 and the template-residual census. Listed in future work with that reason. It becomes a COULD if the census finds more than 150 positives.

### 4.1 Grounding (two-stage, rule-based, no LLM judge)

Every S3 and S4 value must come with a verbatim quote. Stage 1: OCR-tolerant fuzzy locate (rapidfuzz partial ratio >= 90 against page text), recording page and character offsets. Stage 2, support: (a) scope: an institution-level field is supported only by a quote in institution scope (a state-level CD rating must not support the institution's CD rating); (b) value presence: the normalized value appears in the quote; (c) Group C enums: the quote contains a finding predicate ("identified", "lowered", "violation", "substantive") and is not within edit distance k of a stored template; (d) dates and numbers within tolerance. All fields are enums, dates, numbers, sets or index-verified strings, so no entailment judge is needed; this is stated in the README. Groundedness failures are reported in three categories: quote_not_found, quote_out_of_scope, quote_does_not_support.

### 4.2 Traces

Every LLM call is logged as JSONL keyed by (doc_id, field_group, model, prompt_hash) with located-section offsets, response, quote, tokens from the usage fields, cost and latency. `data/traces/gold/` uncompressed; `data/traces/corpus.jsonl.gz` as one file. The replay client raises on a cache miss unless `--allow-api` is set. CI runs on gold traces only and asserts that `results/table1.csv` regenerates byte-identically.

### 4.3 Model plan and cost

Prices from the API reference cached 2026-09-25, stored in `config/prices.yaml` with the retrieval date and verified on run day; tokens are taken from usage fields, not estimates.

- On gold (about 40 documents, located sections): Haiku 4.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 all run; this gives a three-tier accuracy and cost comparison for a few dollars.
- Corpus pass: the cheapest tier within a pre-specified margin of the best tier on dev, via the Batch API (half price). Sonnet 5.5 estimate: about 7.3k input and 1k output tokens per document, 2,700 documents, about $35 in batch; Opus 5.5 about $70 in batch. Locator fallback to whole document (mean about 16k tokens) on 10 percent of documents adds a few dollars.
- Dev and test synchronous runs with reruns: under $20.
- Proposed cap: $150.

## 5. Gold set, census and audit

### 5.1 Gold set (Groups A and B)

- Target 40 documents: 10 dev, 30 test. Fallback at the 10-09 checkpoint: 25 test. Split by bank_id (FieldPoint, for example, has 2024 and 2025 exams); no bank in both splits. The test doc_id list hash is committed before test labeling; the label file hash after.
- Test strata: regulator x method with at least 3 per cell where the corpus has at least 50 documents in the cell, plus 5 NI/SN documents and 4 multi-AA (3 or more) documents. Census positives are not a stratum; if a drawn document is a positive it stays. Strata and selection probabilities in `config/strata.yaml`; gold metrics and corpus estimates are always reported separately.
- Dev: 1 per regulator x method cell plus 4 chosen for layout variety (an FRB Kansas City PE, a multi-state ISB, a non-HMDA bank, an OCC PE with Table O). Dev is never used for inference.
- Group A gold is a one-minute confirmation of the pre-filled index value on the cover page. The test table for Group A is labeled a smoke test (30/30 correct gives a Wilson lower bound of 88.6 percent); the real Group A evaluation is corpus-wide (section 6.3).
- Expected CI widths on test, Wilson 95 percent, n = 30: 30/30 gives [88.6, 100]; 29/30 gives [83.3, 99.4]; 24/30 gives [62.7, 90.5].
- Labeling is blind to system output.

### 5.2 Census (Group C)

1. The locator extracts the review section of every corpus document (2 to 10 lines under one of three headings: OCC "Discriminatory or Other Illegal Credit Practices Review", FDIC the same in capitals, FRB "Fair Lending or Other Illegal Credit Practices Review").
2. Template library `config/review_templates.yaml`: one or more "no evidence" boilerplate paragraphs per regulator, FRB per Reserve Bank district (one PE per district pulled in Phase 1). Residual = section text after removing the best-matching template (rapidfuzz partial ratio >= 90).
3. Flag any section with residual longer than 120 characters or no template match at 85 or above. Hand-read every flagged section (cap 300; above that, add template variants and rerun). Hand-read a random 100 unflagged sections.
4. Label all read sections with the Group C codebook; positives get the full attribute set and are all double-read.
5. Report: count of positives (exact), prevalence with a Wilson CI (30 of 2,700 gives [0.78, 1.58] percent), miss-rate bound from the random 100 (0/100 gives an upper bound of 3.7 percent), and S0, S3, S4, S5 precision and recall on finding_present and rating_lowered against the census. Locator misses are listed and hand-located if fewer than 100, otherwise sampled 50 with the miss rate reported.

Analysis plan wording: "Group C is reported as counts with exact binomial CIs over the labeled census. No recall claim is made beyond the random-sample miss bound. Group C is excluded from the test-set table and from the audit."

### 5.3 Audit (Group B, corpus split, dev and test excluded)

- Unit = document; all Group B fields labeled per document (about 9 values).
- Strata = document-risk tertiles, where document risk = sum of risk flags (section 6.5) over Group B values; tertile cuts on the corpus distribution. Regulator is a post-stratification covariate, reported but not stratified.
- Allocation: 10 documents per tertile, 30 documents, about 270 values (90 per tertile). Design weights N_h / n_h at document level; values inherit the document's weight.
- Blinding: low tertile re-extracted blind (no model value or quote shown); high tertile with model output shown; middle tertile alternating by doc_id parity. Minutes per value recorded in both modes.
- Expected widths per tertile (Wilson, 90 values): 0 errors gives an upper bound of 4.1 percent; 1 error gives [0.2, 6.0]; 10 errors gives [6.1, 19.3]. Overall design-weighted error with a stratified bootstrap CI (1,000 resamples by document within stratum). Per-field error reported with weights, without per-field-per-tertile CIs.

### 5.4 Reliability

Preferred: an outside coder double-codes 8 documents on the hard Group B fields (n_assessment_areas, n_full_scope, loan_products_evaluated, lending_data_period) plus all census positives and 20 matched template-negative sections; Cohen's kappa where both classes occur, percent agreement with prevalence otherwise. Fallback: the author blind re-codes 6 dev documents at least 5 days after first coding, reported as intra-rater stability and labeled as the weaker check.

### 5.5 Labeling hours (estimate)

Dev 10 x 30 min = 5 h; test 30 x 30 min = 15 h; locator check 100 x 1 min = 1.7 h; census flagged (cap 300) x 1 min plus random 100 x 1 min plus positives (about 40) x 5 min = 6 to 10 h; audit 30 x 12 min = 6 h; reliability 2 h. Total 36 to 40 h worst case, about 32 expected, spread over 10-07 to 10-17. Checkpoint 10-09: if dev labeling exceeds 35 minutes per document, test = 25 documents, audit = 24, census cap = 200.

## 6. Evaluation protocol

Pre-registered in `docs/analysis_plan.md`, tagged `plan-v1.0` before test labeling, with `docs/deviations.md` for any change after the tag (before-and-after metrics for any post-run label correction).

### 6.1 Work rule

Pilot on dev, measure, fix rules and prompts (versioned), freeze, label test blind, evaluate test once, scale to corpus, audit. `evaluate.py` refuses to run on split == test before the tag exists, logs every test invocation, and refuses a second test run without a deviations entry.

### 6.2 Metric types and scoring matrix

Each field in `fields.yaml` has `metric_type` in {accuracy, binary_pr, set_pr} and a `tolerance`. Scoring matrix:

| gold \ system | value | absent | abstain |
|---|---|---|---|
| value | correct or wrong by matching rule (wrong = hallucination) | wrong (fabricated absence; hallucination) | abstention |
| not_applicable | fabrication | correct null | abstention |
| absent_from_document | fabrication | correct null | abstention |

Fabrication rate denominator = gold NA or ABS items per field, reported alongside the rate. Abstentions are excluded from conditional error and counted in the abstention rate. Precision and recall are reported separately with Wilson 95 percent CIs; F1 is secondary. Error rates are reported overall and conditional on the system answering.

### 6.3 Group A at corpus scale

For every corpus document, S1, S3 and S5 outputs are compared with the index: accuracy, abstention and fabrication per regulator x method x release year with Wilson CIs (cells of 50 to 250 documents). Disagreements are triaged into our error, source error, or definitional difference, and feed the data-quality table.

### 6.4 Correctness x groundedness

Per field and system: the 2x2 cells of correctness x groundedness, the three groundedness failure categories, abstention, and the derived hallucination rate (wrong value OR misgrounded quote).

### 6.5 Risk score, calibration, review curve

Flags per value (0 to 4): locator_miss for the field's section; S1 and S3 disagreement where both answer; any groundedness failure; consistency failure (lending == overall for OCC Small; period_end <= exam_date; n_full_scope <= n_assessment_areas; rating_after == overall_rating when lowered at overall level). No fitted weights. Calibration: 5 bins on the Group B test values, ECE with a bootstrapped SE, plot labeled indicative; Group A corpus-wide index agreement validates the flags at scale; the audit verifies with design weights. Test and audit are never pooled for calibration. Review curve: residual error vs share of values reviewed, ordered by flag count; Group A curve exact corpus-wide, Group B curve from the audit; the manual-review error rate from the reliability check is marked on the plot.

### 6.6 Error-cause table

Multiple causes allowed, attributed to the stage that produced them: parsing, locator miss (an S1 error, not an S3 hallucination), wrong scope (state or AA instead of institution), wrong period, unit or format, boilerplate confusion, reasoning, fabrication.

### 6.7 Heterogeneity

Group A: regulator x method x year on the corpus. Group B: by regulator on test. The scanned vs born-digital cut is dropped unless at least 20 scanned documents exist, and the README says so.

### 6.8 Leakage and contamination

Split by bank_id everywhere. Rules and prompts are tuned on dev, the 100-document locator check, and corpus-wide index agreement, never on test. Closed-book contamination baseline: S3 given only bank name and exam date for the test documents, asked for overall rating and finding_present; accuracy reported in Limitations as a contamination bound.

### 6.9 Cost

Manual baseline = the author's own per-field timer during gold labeling at a stated wage (not an assumed pages-per-hour figure). Reported: tokens per document under located vs whole-document input; locator fallback rate on test and corpus; cost per document and per correct field by system and model tier; corpus extrapolation; total cost at three review operating points (top 10, 25 and 50 percent of values by risk) using reviewer minutes per item from the audit.

### 6.10 Locator check

100 random corpus documents including one PE per Reserve Bank district: for four sections (cover and rating, description of institution, review section, AA concentration and LTD table) record found or not found. Locator recall per section type is reported before any LLM metric.

## 7. Descriptive analysis and memo (explicitly non-causal)

- Decomposition of NI and SN ratings into performance-driven vs compliance-driven (substantive_lowered), counts by regulator and method with exact binomial CIs, no comparative language across regulators.
- Findings registry `results/findings_registry.csv` (bank, regulator, dates, finding_type, laws_cited, practice_basis, rating_before and after, finding_source).
- FRB ISB matrix-floor check: an overall rating below the floor implied by the lending and CD ratings in the FRB CSV should imply rating_effect = substantive_lowered; agreements and disagreements reported with root cause.
- Rating transition matrix (prior_rating to overall_rating) and exam cycle length by prior rating.
- Publication lag (release_date minus exam_date) by regulator and rating.
- Data-quality findings `results/data_quality_findings.csv` (source, field, n_affected, example_doc, root_cause in {our_error, source_error, definitional}, recommendation). Seed rows to verify: FDIC ISB lending rating apparently reported on the Large Bank scale (161 of 167 ISB rows "High Satisfactory"; to be confirmed against at least three gold PEs before it appears in the README); FDIC release-month-only dates; FRB duplicate MSA and State rows; OCC 2026 rows in the 2023-2025 pull; OCC absent ISB component ratings in the index; typos in source documents.
- Memo `docs/memo.md`, two pages: a numbered list of fields to publish in structured form, each with the regulator that already prints it, the discrepancy observed in the current feed, and the cost of continuing to extract it from PDFs. Priority order: ISB component ratings; exam date in the FDIC API; a structured flag for illegal-credit-practice findings, laws and downgrades; inside-AA share and LTD; LMI share last (regulators can compute it from HMDA).
